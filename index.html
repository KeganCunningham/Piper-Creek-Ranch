Discover Columbus, Texas

A charming historic town with Southern character — just minutes from Piper Creek Ranch Estates.

~15 min from the property
⛩

Historic Courthouse Square

The 1891 Colorado County Courthouse features a Tiffany-style stained glass dome and copper Neo-classic cupola. The surrounding square is one of the most prominent historic districts in Texas.

♫

Stafford Opera House

Built in 1886, the only flat-floored opera house still standing in Texas. Hosts monthly dinner theater, live performances, weddings, and community events.

🍷

Local Craft Scene

Explore Hound Song Brewing Company, Whistling Duck Winery, and eclectic shopping on the square. The Columbus Country Market runs Saturdays, March through November.

🏝

Colorado River & Outdoors

The 6.5-mile Columbus Paddling Trail offers kayaking through stunning scenery. Columbus is also home to Grandma’s Oak, the largest live oak on the Texas Big Tree Registry.
